Limits by mechanism

Starting points to tune from, not final values.

Read How the limits work first. The values below are conservative starting points for common FRC mechanisms. Set them, run a practice match, then adjust: raise the stator limit only if a move cannot finish and the motor is not hot; keep the supply limit at or below the branch breaker.

MechanismStator (start)Supply (start)Why
Drivetrain / traction80 ABreaker ratingWants torque, but is limited by traction and the breaker.
Swerve azimuth (steering)40 A40 ALight, fast moves; rarely needs high torque.
Elevator / arm60 A40 AEnough to lift the load; hold with a limit or a brake.
Intake / feeder40 A30 ALow continuous draw; protect against a jam.

Tuning from the start point

  • The move is sluggish or stalls short. Raise the stator limit in small steps until it completes, then stop.
  • The motor gets hot or folds. The mechanism is drawing more than it needs — lower the stator limit, or gear the mechanism down. Power & thermal budget
  • The breaker trips. Lower the supply limit below the breaker rating.
Warning

A mechanism that can jam (intake, roller, arm against a hard stop) needs a stator limit low enough that a stalled motor does not cook itself before you notice. Do not leave it at the ceiling.
