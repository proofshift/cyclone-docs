Install & connect

Configure and tune a Cyclone from a browser over USB-C, or on the SystemCore over CAN.

SWYFT Link is the tool for setting up Cyclones. In a browser it drives one motor over USB-C. On the 2027 SystemCore it reaches every Cyclone on the CAN bus. Both give the same pages: Control, Config, Health, and Firmware.

Connect over USB-C

  1. Plug a Cyclone into your laptop with USB-C.
  2. Open SWYFT Link in a supported browser. It detects the motor on its own — there is no connect step.
  3. The motor’s power leads must be energised (6–24 V) for it to spin; USB-C alone does not power the windings.

Install on the SystemCore

Download the package: SWYFT Link 2026.08.29.ipk

  1. Open the SystemCore home page in a browser and select Add package.
  2. Drop the .ipk onto the box, or select it from a folder. It installs on its own.
  3. Open the SWYFT Link tile from the home page.
  4. Cyclones on the CAN bus appear in the device list.
Note

Anything you can do over USB-C you can do over CAN, and the reverse — the two are equal control paths. Firmware recovery (DFU) is the exception: it runs over USB-C. Firmware & DFU
