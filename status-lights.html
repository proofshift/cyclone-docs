Status lights

Seven lights: two report the controller, five are yours.

Under the rear window are seven lights in a row. The two at the ends report controller state and cannot be written by robot code. The five in the middle, numbered 1–5 from left to right, are yours to set. A separate LED beside the DFU pinhole indicates bus power. Wiring

Tap the lights to play the pattern.

Your five lights

Set the middle five from robot code, from SWYFT Link, or from the console. Send colour channels 0–255; the motor caps the brightest channel and scales the rest, so pure white and a dim tint both read cleanly. Colour holds until power is lost, then comes up dark until set again. Setting all five to black hands them back to the controller, which is not the same as off — clearLEDs() is the off path. LEDs in the API reference

Warning

A latched fault always paints the two end lights, and your five cannot override it. Do not rely on the middle five as a driver’s only signal.

Animations

Instead of a solid colour you can run one animation across the five: Static, ChaseForward, ChaseReverse, Breathe, or Rainbow. Colour and animation are exclusive — setting a colour stops an animation.
