Faults & errors

A hard fault latches and cuts output. A warning stays live and does not.

The Cyclone reports two kinds of condition. A hard fault latches, de-energises the bridge, and holds the motor off until the cause clears and you clear the fault. A warning flags a condition without stopping the motor. The end lights show a latched fault as red and orange alternating. Status lights

Hard faults

FaultConditionPossible fix
Under-voltageBus below the lockout. The motor will not arm.Charge or swap the battery; check the breaker and lead connections. USB-C alone does not power the windings.
Over-voltageBus above the trip. Output is cut.Keep the bus within 6–24 V; check the battery count and that nothing drives it above 24 V.
Over-temperatureBoard temperature reached the trip. Output is cut.Let it cool, then lower the current limit or gear down so it stays out of the thermal fold.
EncoderShaft angle became untrustworthy while driving.Power-cycle the motor. If it repeats, the motor needs service — contact support.
Gate driverThe power stage reported its own fault.Power-cycle the motor. If it repeats, take it out of service and contact support.
Over-currentCurrent exceeded the firmware limit.Clear any mechanical jam. Lower the load, or raise the stator limit if the motor is not hot.
CommunicationThe command or heartbeat link went silent past its watchdog.Check CAN wiring and terminators, or the USB-C cable; confirm the robot program is running and enabled.
Over-speedThe rotor ran past its ceiling.Command a lower speed; check the gearing and that the mechanism is not back-driving the motor.
Bus sagThe bus collapsed under load.Check battery health and the main connections; lower the supply limit; use adequate-gauge leads.
StallThe velocity loop could not move the shaft.Clear the jam. Raise the stator limit if the motor is not hot, or gear down for more torque.
E-stopA pit or host kill latched the motor off.Release the e-stop (releaseEstop()), then clear any remaining fault.

Warnings

Warnings do not stop the motor. They report that a hard fault is near, or that something upstream is degraded: temperature approaching the trip, the bus below the lockout right now (as on USB-C with no battery), CAN traffic dropping, or the enable heartbeat gone stale.

Clearing a fault

  1. Read the active fault in SWYFT Link, on the Health page.
  2. Fix the cause — cool the motor, restore the bus, fix the wiring.
  3. Clear the fault. From robot code, clearFaults(); it is refused while the cause is still present.

An e-stop is separate: it holds the motor off and refuses to re-arm until you release it (releaseEstop()), then clear any remaining fault. Control requests
