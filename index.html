Specifications

Electrical

Bus voltage and the currents the motor draws and limits.

ParameterValue
Nominal bus12 V (3-cell / FRC)
Operating voltage6–24 V. The motor will not arm below 6 V and cuts output above 24 V.
Continuous current40–60 A
Stator current limitConfigurable, default 60 A, up to 160 A
Supply current limitConfigurable, default 40 A, up to 60 A

Two currents matter, and they are not the same. Stator current flows in the windings and sets torque; supply current is what the battery delivers. Duty cycle links them — the supply draws roughly stator × duty, so a large winding current pulls only a small battery current at low duty. Near stall at about 10 % duty, ~70 A in the windings is only ~7 A from the pack. Set the stator limit to protect the motor and the supply limit to protect the battery and breaker; one does not replace the other.

Performance

Speed and torque at the ends of the operating range.

ParameterValue
Free speed7530 RPM
Stall torque3.8 N·m
Stall current275 A

Speed and torque trade off. Free speed (7530 RPM) is the no-load ceiling and scales with bus voltage; torque climbs with stator current to the 3.8 N·m stall figure at 275 A. Holding a heavier load costs speed and draws more current. Stall is a modeled peak, not a place to live — the thermal system pulls you out of it.

Control and sensing

How the motor commutates, reads shaft position, and takes commands.

ParameterValue
CommutationTrapezoidal
Encoder14-bit absolute magnetic, 16,384 counts per turn
Duty outputSigned fraction of bus, −1 to 1
Closed-loop modesCurrent, velocity, position Under construction

The controller energises two of the three phases at a time and steps through six combinations per electrical revolution, reading the 14-bit encoder to choose the step and its timing. Duty cycle sets how much of the bus each step applies, from −1 (full reverse) to +1 (full forward). Closed-loop current, velocity, and position build on this and are still under construction.

Thermal

How the motor protects itself as it heats up.

ParameterValue
Over-temperature warning75 °C default, configurable 40–80 °C
Over-temperature trip85 °C. Output is cut.
Thermal foldCurrent is reduced as the thermal budget fills, before the trip

The controller tracks heat with a current-squared-time (i²t) budget: brief high current is fine, sustained high current fills it. Past a threshold it folds the current limit down in proportion — trading torque for temperature — and cuts output entirely at the 85 °C trip. Sizing the current limits to the job is what keeps a mechanism out of the fold.
