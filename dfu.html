Firmware & DFU

Update firmware over USB-C, and recover a motor that will not enumerate.

Update firmware

Open the Firmware page in SWYFT Link and flash an update over USB-C or CAN. It shows the installed version; the status lights go solid white during the transfer, then the motor reboots.

Warning

Do not remove power or unplug USB-C while the lights are solid white. If a flash is interrupted and the motor will not enumerate afterward, use DFU recovery below.

DFU recovery

Use DFU only when a motor will not enumerate — usually after an interrupted flash. It runs over USB-C.

  1. Remove all power from the leads first — no battery, no PDH, no bench supply.
  2. Hold the DFU button.
  3. While holding, connect USB-C. Wait until the power LED beside the DFU pinhole lights from USB alone.
  4. Release the button.
  5. Flash the image from the Firmware page.
  6. Disconnect USB-C, power the motor normally, and confirm its identity in SWYFT Link.
