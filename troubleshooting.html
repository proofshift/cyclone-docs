Troubleshooting

Start at the status lights — they name the problem before the code does.

Read the two end lights first (Status lights), then match the symptom below.

The motor will not move

Tap the lights to play the pattern.

SymptomCauseFix
DarkNo powerCheck the leads, polarity, and the breaker.
Red, alternatingNothing is talking to itSeat USB-C, or fix CAN wiring and add trunk terminators.
Orange, alternatingOn the bus, no program addressing itDeploy code; confirm the device number is constructed in code.
Orange, togetherRobot disabledEnable in the Driver Station.
Orange, solidNo command arrivingSend a command — duty, velocity, position, or current.
Blue, flashing togetherDuplicate device numberGive one motor a different number (1–62).

It moves, but wrong

SymptomCauseFix
Turns the wrong wayDirection not setInvert in SWYFT Link, not in code. Orientation
Weak or stalls shortStator limit too lowRaise the stator limit in small steps. Current limits
Loses torque under loadThermal foldGear down or lower the limit so it does not overheat. Power & thermal budget
Breaker tripsSupply draw above the breakerLower the supply limit below the breaker rating.

USB-C or CAN will not connect

  • USB-C not detected: try a data-capable cable and a different port. The motor enumerates as a serial device with no driver — nothing to install.
  • CAN device missing: confirm H/L are not swapped, the trunk is terminated at both ends, and the device number is unique and in range (1–62).
  • Will not enumerate after a flash: recover with DFU.

For a latched fault (red and orange alternating), see Faults & errors.
